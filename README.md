## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **full-stack engineer** based in Addis Ababa, building **production software end-to-end** — from Flutter mobile apps and React/Next.js web frontends to NestJS/Express backends and the databases behind them.

My work focuses on systems people actually rely on: **payment integrations** (telebirr mobile money), **national-identity / eKYC** flows (Fayda integration), **inventory & sales management (ERP/POS)** platforms, and **multi-platform SaaS products**. I care about clean architecture, shipping complete features, and solving real business problems — not just writing code.

- 🔭 Shipping multi-platform products (mobile + web + dashboard + API) as cohesive systems
- 🧩 Comfortable across the stack: UI, API design, data modeling, and integrations
- 🌍 Deep experience with **Ethiopia-specific fintech & gov-tech integrations**
- 🤝 **Open to new opportunities, freelance, and collaborations**
